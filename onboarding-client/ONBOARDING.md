# Vallecito Partner Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Vallecito! To get your agency or sanctuary set up with a beautiful, fully integrated standalone webpage on our platform, we need some details from you.

## 1. Brand Identity & Theme
Please provide the following to customize the look and feel of your page:
- **Logo**: A high-resolution image of your logo (PNG with transparent background preferred).
- **Cover Photo**: A high-quality landscape image used for the top hero section.
- **Primary Color**: Your brand's main color (Hex code, e.g., `#0E0E0E`).
- **Accent Color**: Your brand's secondary/highlight color (Hex code, e.g., `#C9A24D`).
- **Fonts**: Your preferred Google Fonts (1 Display font for titles, 1 Body font for text).

## 2. Agency / Sanctuary Information
Basic details about your organization:
- **Agency Name**
- **Tagline / Short Slogan**
- **Description / Lineage**: A brief summary of your history and what you do.
- **Contact Phone** (WhatsApp number)
- **Contact Email**
- **Category**: (Expedition Agency or Therapist/Sanctuary)

## 3. The Ethos (Why Choose Us?)
We feature a section that highlights your unique value proposition.
- **Vision/Mission Statement**: A short paragraph explaining your vision, mission, and values.
- **3 Key Pillars**: Three short bullet points. For each, provide:
  - Title (e.g., "Native Guides")
  - Short Description (e.g., "Expert locals rooted in the Andes.")
  - Recommended Icon (e.g., Compass, Heart, Mountain, Target, Clock)

## 4. Offerings (Packages / Therapies)
For each experience, tour, or treatment you offer, please provide:
- **Title**: Name of the experience.
- **Tagline**: A short, catchy subtitle.
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of the offering.
- **Duration**: (e.g., "3 Days / 2 Nights" or "90 minutes").
- **Difficulty**: (e.g., "Easy", "Moderate", "Challenging").
- **Base Price (USD)**: Price per person.
- **Package Type**: (Single Day, Multi-Day, or Treatment).
- **Inclusions / Exclusions**: What is included and what is not.
- **Daily Itinerary**: A day-by-day breakdown (for multi-day tours).

## 5. Image Assets (Google Drive)
Please provide a link to a Google Drive folder organized as follows:
- `brand/`: Logo and Cover Photo.
- `packages/`: Create a subfolder for each package (e.g., `packages/ausangate-trek/`).
  - Inside each package folder, provide 1 **Hero Image** (named `hero.jpg`) and 3-10 **Gallery Images** (`gallery-1.jpg`, etc.).

---
*Once you provide this information, our automated system will seamlessly generate your branded webpage and integrate your offerings into the Vallecito travel design platform!*
