# Expat & Digital Nomad Resources: Cusco & Sacred Valley

This is a curated list of high-quality websites and blogs written by or for expats, digital nomads, and long-term travelers living in Cusco and the Sacred Valley of Peru. These sources are excellent for scraping local knowledge, understanding the expat market for the AI concierge, and discovering local businesses and experiences to add to the app.

### 1. Slight North
* **URL:** [slightnorth.com](https://slightnorth.com/)
* **Content Focus:** "DIY" independent travel guides.
* **Details:** Extremely useful for extracting logistical information on how to explore popular and hidden sites (like Pisac Ruins, Sacsayhuaman, Tipón, Huchuy Qosqo, and Kinsa Cocha) without booking expensive guided tours. Contains detailed breakdowns of local transport (*colectivos*), the Boleto Turístico, and neighborhood guides (like San Blas).

### 2. Find Away Abroad
* **URL:** [findawayabroad.com](https://findawayabroad.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Comprehensive expat moving and living guides.
* **Details:** Highlights Cusco as an affordable, safe destination for digital nomads. Contains deep dives into the cost of living (rent, groceries, transport), top residential neighborhoods (Historic District, San Blas, Magisterio), and very practical tips for finding housing locally (e.g., looking for "Se Alquila" signs and using local classifieds).

### 3. Vagabond Buddha
* **URL:** [vagabondbuddha.com](https://vagabondbuddha.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Retiree and digital nomad cost-of-living breakdowns.
* **Details:** Renowned for highly specific monthly budget estimates, grocery costs, rent expectations, and neighborhood reviews. Great for understanding the financial reality of long-term living in the Sacred Valley (Urubamba, Pisac) versus Cusco city.

### 4. Tales of a Backpacker
* **URL:** [talesofabackpacker.com](https://talesofabackpacker.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Backpacker, solo traveler, and "hippy expat" scene.
* **Details:** Authored by Claire Sturzaker, this blog offers a wealth of recommendations for boutique hotels, party hostels (e.g., Kokopelli, Wild Rover), local food spots (San Pedro Market), and day trips. Also provides important acclimatization and safety tips.

### 5. Stiff Yogi
* **URL:** [stiffyogi.com](https://stiffyogi.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Remote work, wellness, and spiritual tourism in Pisac.
* **Details:** Features detailed accounts of living and working remotely specifically in Pisac. It is a great resource for extracting information about the local yoga scene, plant medicine community, and specific health-conscious cafes and restaurants favored by expats.

### 6. Wolves and Waterfalls
* **URL:** [wolvesandwaterfalls.com](https://wolvesandwaterfalls.com/) (Also heavily associated with Wolf Totem Guesthouse)
* **Content Focus:** Solo female travel and boutique experiences.
* **Details:** Authored by Allison Wolf, this brand focuses on curated female travel. It provides great guides for Pisac ruins, local markets, and highly recommends specific boutique accommodations like the Wolf Totem Guesthouse in the Sacred Valley.

### 7. The Doe
* **URL:** [thedoe.com](https://thedoe.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Critical essays and reflections on expat life.
* **Details:** Offers a more reflective look at living in the Sacred Valley, discussing "expat privilege," the ethics of spiritual tourism, and the complexities of integrating with the local Quechua-speaking communities. Good for feeding the AI concierge a more nuanced understanding of local dynamics.

### 8. The Broke Backpacker
* **URL:** [thebrokebackpacker.com](https://thebrokebackpacker.com/)
* **Content Focus:** Budget travel, visas, and raw logistics.
* **Details:** An essential resource for understanding visa situations (tourist visas vs. Digital Nomad visas, border runs, extensions up to 183 days), transport logistics, and raw budget travel tips for navigating the Cusco region long-term.
