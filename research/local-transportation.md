# Local Transportation in the Sacred Valley

This document outlines the primary methods of local transportation (colectivos, buses, and taxis) between Cusco and the Sacred Valley towns (Urubamba, Pisac, Calca, Ollantaytambo, etc.), which is crucial for Concierge recommendations and the `TRANSPORT` category logistics.

## 1. Cusco to Urubamba (via Chinchero)
The most common route for getting to the heart of the Sacred Valley.

*   **Departure Point (Cusco):** **Calle Pavitos** (near the intersection with Avenida Grau). It's a 5–10 minute taxi ride from Plaza de Armas (S/ 5–8) or a short walk from the San Pedro market.
*   **Arrival Point (Urubamba):** Terminal Terrestre (main bus station) or along Avenida Mariscal Castilla.
*   **Cost:** S/ 6 to S/ 10 (approx. $2–$3 USD).
*   **Duration:** 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes.
*   **Schedule:** No fixed schedule. Colectivos leave when full (usually 10–15 passengers). They run frequently from roughly 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

## 2. Cusco to Pisac & Calca
If you are heading to Pisac or further down the valley to Calca, you must go to a different terminal in Cusco.

*   **Departure Point (Cusco):** **Calle Puputi** (about a 15-minute walk or a short taxi ride from the historic center).
*   **Arrival Point (Pisac):** Near the bridge at the entrance to Pisac town.
*   **Arrival Point (Calca):** Terminal in Calca (if staying on the colectivo past Pisac).
*   **Cost:** S/ 4 to S/ 6 to Pisac. Slightly more to Calca (approx S/ 6 to S/ 8).
*   **Duration:** About 45 minutes to 1 hour to Pisac.

## 3. Cusco to Ollantaytambo
Ollantaytambo is the main gateway for catching the train to Machu Picchu.

*   **Departure Point (Cusco):** **Calle Pavitos** (shared with the Urubamba colectivos, but you look for vans specifically shouting "Ollanta").
*   **Arrival Point (Ollantaytambo):** Near the Plaza de Armas or the main road leading down to the train station.
*   **Cost:** S/ 10 to S/ 15 (approx. $3–$4 USD).
*   **Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours.

## 4. Urubamba Bridge (Paradero Puente) to Ollantaytambo / Machu Picchu
If you are staying in Urubamba and need to get to the Ollantaytambo train station for Machu Picchu, you don't need a private taxi.

*   **Departure Point (Urubamba):** **Paradero Puente** (the main bridge crossing the Vilcanota River at the edge of Urubamba).
*   **Transport Type:** Omnibus (large local buses) or passing colectivos. 
*   **Arrival Point (Ollantaytambo):** The main road in Ollantaytambo, a short walk or Tuk-Tuk ride from the train station.
*   **Cost:** S/ 2 to S/ 4.
*   **Duration:** 25 to 30 minutes.
*   **Pro Tip:** This is the cheapest and most authentic way to connect from the center of the Sacred Valley to the Machu Picchu train network.

## 5. General Combi/Colectivo Rules
*   **Payment:** Pay the driver or the "cobrador" (the assistant) in cash (soles) either before departure or upon arrival. Carry small bills and coins.
*   **Luggage:** Colectivos (shared minivans) have limited space. If you have large suitcases, you might be asked to pay for an extra seat or have the bags strapped to the roof.
*   **Safety & Comfort:** They are generally safe and used by locals daily, but can be cramped. They make stops along the way to pick up/drop off passengers.

## 6. Airports & Connections
*   **Alejandro Velasco Astete Airport (CUZ):** The main arrival point. There are NO direct colectivos from the airport to the Sacred Valley. 
*   **Connection:** You must take a private taxi from the airport either directly to your hotel in the Sacred Valley (expensive, $20-$40), OR take a 10-minute taxi from the airport to **Calle Pavitos** or **Calle Puputi** to catch a colectivo (budget-friendly).
