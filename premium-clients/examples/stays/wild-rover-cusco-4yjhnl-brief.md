# UnlockCusco - One-Page Website Strategy: Wild Rover

## 1. Defining Your Brand Identity
- **Primary Brand Color (Hex)**: `#4CAF50` (Vibrant Green)
- **Secondary Accent Color (Hex)**: `#FFC107` (Energetic Amber)
- **Typography (Google Fonts)**:
    - Display: `Montserrat`
    - Body: `Open Sans`

## 2. Business Information
- **Business Name**: Wild Rover
- **Tagline / Short Slogan**: Your Social Adventure Hub in Cusco. Views & Vibes Await!
- **Description / Lineage**: Wild Rover has established itself as a premier social hostel in Cusco, renowned for its lively atmosphere, fantastic staff, and engaging activities. We're more than just a place to stay; we're a community where travelers connect, share experiences, and create unforgettable memories, all while enjoying breathtaking city views.
- **Category**: STAYS

## 3. The Ethos (Why Choose Us?)
- **Vision/Mission Statement**: To foster a vibrant, inclusive community where every traveler feels welcomed, inspired, and connected, offering unforgettable experiences and a base for exploring the wonders of Cusco.
- **3 Key Pillars**:
    - **Unrivaled Social Scene**
        - Short Description: Cultivating connections through daily activities, lively common areas, and famous mixology classes, ensuring every guest finds their tribe.
        - Recommended Icon: `party-popper`
    - **Exceptional Host Team**
        - Short Description: Our dedicated and friendly staff go above and beyond, from warm welcomes at reception to leading engaging events, making your stay seamless and fun.
        - Recommended Icon: `users`
    - **Iconic Views & Vibrant Location**
        - Short Description: Perched with panoramic vistas of Cusco, our prime location offers easy access to the city's hotspots, rewarding your journey with breathtaking scenery.
        - Recommended Icon: `mountain`
