## 4. Offerings
- **Title**: Cuy al Horno (Baked Guinea Pig)
- **Tagline**: A Traditional Delicacy, Masterfully Prepared
- **Description**: Our celebrated whole baked guinea pig, slow-cooked for an hour in our wood-fired oven until its skin is perfectly crispy and the meat succulent. A quintessential taste of Andean tradition and a must-try for any visitor.
- **Base Price**: S/ 85
- **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Wood-fired perfection
    - Crispy skin, tender meat
    - Authentic Peruvian experience
    - Generous portion, typically served with local potatoes and sauce

- **Title**: Lomo Saltado Tradicional
- **Tagline**: Classic Peruvian Stir-Fry, Reimagined
- **Description**: A vibrant and hearty Peruvian classic featuring tender beef strips, stir-fried with onions, tomatoes, and aji amarillo. Served alongside perfectly crispy fries and fluffy white rice, embodying a harmonious blend of flavors.
- **Base Price**: S/ 70
- **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Classic comfort food
    - Rich, savory flavors
    - Fresh, locally sourced ingredients
    - Balanced and satisfying meal

- **Title**: Aji de Gallina Premium
- **Tagline**: Creamy Chicken Stew, Comforting & Flavorful
- **Description**: Shredded chicken simmered in a luxurious, creamy yellow chili pepper sauce, enriched with cheese and nuts. Elegantly presented with slices of potato, black olives, and a hard-boiled egg. A beloved Peruvian staple offering comforting warmth.
- **Base Price**: S/ 65
- **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Velvety, creamy texture
    - Mildly spiced, rich flavor profile
    - Soul-warming and traditional recipe
    - Beautifully garnished presentation

- **Title**: Pachapapa Andean Platter
- **Tagline**: A Culinary Journey Through the Andes
- **Description**: A delightful and shareable selection of local appetizers, artisanal cheeses, and cured meats, complemented by Andean maize and fresh dips. Perfect for experiencing a variety of authentic Peruvian flavors with friends and family.
- **Base Price**: S/ 90
- **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Ideal for sharing

    - Diverse flavors and textures
    - Excellent accompaniment to our signature cocktails
