# UnlockCusco - One-Page Website Strategy: Ollantaytambo Archaeological Site

## 1. Defining Your Brand Identity
- **Primary Brand Color (Hex)**: `#B06500` (A deep, earthy ochre, reminiscent of ancient stones and sun-baked landscapes)
- **Secondary Accent Color (Hex)**: `#4682B4` (A vibrant steel blue, evoking the Andean sky and local textiles)
- **Typography (Google Fonts)**:
    - **Display Font**: Lora (Elegant serif with a historical, yet readable feel)
    - **Body Font**: Open Sans (Clean, modern sans-serif for optimal readability)

## 2. Business Information
- **Business Name**: Ollantaytambo Archaeological Site
- **Tagline / Short Slogan**: Journey Through Inca Majesty: Fortress, Terraces, and Timeless Views
- **Description / Lineage**: An imposing Inca archaeological complex nestled in the Sacred Valley, Ollantaytambo served as a strategic fortress, ceremonial center, and agricultural hub. Its monumental terraces, well-preserved structures, and enigmatic stone carvings offer a profound glimpse into the advanced engineering and spiritual world of the Inca Empire.
- **Category**: CULTURE

## 3. The Ethos (Why Choose Us?)
- **Vision/Mission Statement**: Our vision is to preserve and share the profound legacy of the Inca civilization, inviting visitors to intimately connect with the history, engineering marvels, and spiritual grandeur of Ollantaytambo, fostering a deeper appreciation for ancient Andean culture and its enduring spirit.
- **3 Key Pillars**:
    - **Historical Immersion**
        - **Short Description**: Dive deep into the captivating narratives and architectural wonders of an ancient Inca fortress and ceremonial center.
        - **Recommended Icon**: `book-open`
    - **Panoramic Majesty**
        - **Short Description**: Ascend to awe-inspiring viewpoints offering unparalleled perspectives of the Sacred Valley and surrounding peaks.
        - **Recommended Icon**: `mountain`
    - **Authentic Andean Connection**
        - **Short Description**: Connect with the vibrant local culture and artisan traditions surrounding this monumental historic site.
        - **Recommended Icon**: `users`

## 4. Offerings
(Visitor Experiences)
- **Title**: Self-Guided Exploration
    - **Tagline**: Discover the ancient marvels at your own pace.
    - **Description**: Gain full access to the entire archaeological park, including the iconic Temple of the Sun, impressive terraces, and residential areas. Ideal for independent explorers who wish to immerse themselves without a fixed schedule.
    - **Base Price**: ~$25 USD
    - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
        -   Full access to all accessible areas of the archaeological complex
        -   Flexibility to explore independently
        -   Breathtaking photo opportunities at every turn

- **Title**: Expert-Led Historical Journey
    - **Tagline**: Unravel the secrets with a knowledgeable local guide.
    - **Description**: A comprehensive and engaging tour led by certified local guides, offering in-depth explanations of the site's history, architectural significance, and cultural context. Perfect for those seeking a deeper understanding.
    - **Base Price**: ~$50 USD
    - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
        -   Certified local guide expertise and insights
        -   Detailed historical and cultural narratives
        -   Interactive Q&A sessions
        -   Personalized recommendations for further exploration

- **Title**: Sacred Valley Vista Experience
    - **Tagline**: Witness Ollantaytambo bathed in the golden light of dawn or dusk.
    - **Description**: An exclusive, early morning or late afternoon visit, designed for a more serene and contemplative experience. Enjoy the site with fewer crowds and capture stunning photographs as the sun illuminates the ancient stones.
    - **Base Price**: ~$85 USD
    - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
        -   Exclusive access during non-peak hours
        -   Photographer-friendly lighting conditions
        -   Tranquil atmosphere for deeper reflection
        -   Optional light refreshments available
