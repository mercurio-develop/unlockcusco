## 2. Business Information
- **Business Name**: Ollantaytambo Museum
- **Tagline / Short Slogan**: Uncover Ancient Inka Mysteries in the Sacred Valley
- **Description / Lineage**: Nestled within the breathtaking Sacred Valley, the Ollantaytambo Museum offers an unparalleled journey into the heart of the Inka civilization. Discover the architectural marvels, ancient rituals, and enigmatic history surrounding the iconic Sun Temple and the majestic terraces of Ollantaytambo.
