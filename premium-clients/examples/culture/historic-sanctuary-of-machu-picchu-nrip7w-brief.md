## 2. Business Information
- **Business Name**: Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu
- **Tagline / Short Slogan**: Unveiling the Timeless Majesty of the Incas.
- **Description / Lineage**: A UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World, the Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu stands as an unparalleled testament to Inca civilization. Nestled high in the Andes mountains of Peru, this ancient citadel, built around 1450 AD, showcases masterful engineering, profound astronomical alignment, and a rich cultural heritage that continues to captivate visitors from across the globe. It offers an immersive journey into a sacred past.
- **Category**: CULTURE

We strive to provide an awe-inspiring, respectful, and educational experience that honors its sacred heritage and leaves an indelible mark on every visitor.

## 4. Offerings

  - **Tagline**: Discover the heart of Machu Picchu's grandeur.
  - **Description**: The most popular and comprehensive route, Circuit 2, guides you through the iconic main plaza, Temple of the Sun, Intihuatana, and Sacred Rock. Experience the architectural marvels and panoramic vistas that define Machu Picchu. Ideal for first-time visitors seeking the quintessential experience.
  - **Base Price**: $180
  - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Official entry ticket for Circuit 2.
    - Round-trip bus transfer from Aguas Calientes to Machu Picchu entrance.
    - Expert certified group guide (approx. 2.5 hours).
    - Informative historical context and insights.
    - Access to prime photographic viewpoints.

- **Title**: Mountain Ascent Challenge (Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain)
  - **Tagline**: Elevate your perspective with an unforgettable climb.
  - **Description**: For the adventurous spirit, this experience combines the Classic Citadel Immersion with an exhilarating climb up either Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain. Gain unparalleled panoramic views of the entire sanctuary and surrounding Andean peaks after a rewarding trek.
  - **Base Price**: $250
  - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Official entry ticket for Circuit 2 + Mountain (Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain).
    - Round-trip bus transfer from Aguas Calientes to Machu Picchu entrance.
    - Expert certified group guide for the main citadel (approx. 2.5 hours).
    - Dedicated time for mountain ascent (un-guided for safety, but with clear path).
    - Exclusive high-altitude photographic opportunities.

- **Title**: Bespoke Private Discovery Tour
  - **Tagline**: Explore at your own pace with personalized expert guidance.
  - **Description**: Enjoy the ultimate flexibility and depth with a dedicated private guide. Perfect for those desiring a personalized, unhurried, and intimate experience.
  - **Base Price**: $400
  - **Highlights/Inclusions**:
    - Official entry ticket for Circuit of choice (e.g., Circuit 2 or Circuit 3).
    - Round-trip bus transfer from Aguas Calientes to Machu Picchu entrance.
    - Private, expert certified guide dedicated exclusively to your party (approx. 3-4 hours).
    - Customizable itinerary and pace to suit your preferences.
    - In-depth historical narratives and cultural insights.
    - Priority access and seamless logistics.
