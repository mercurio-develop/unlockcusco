# Travel App Competitor Matrix

This document provides context on the major players in the travel and experience booking space to guide competitive analysis for Vallecito.

## Wanderlog
**Focus:** Free itinerary planning, map integration, and collaborative travel.
**Target Audience:** DIY travelers organizing complex, multi-day trips with friends.
**Key Features:** Interactive maps tied to a day-by-day itinerary, drag-and-drop place reordering, budgeting tools, email flight/hotel parsing.
**Monetization:** Freemium (Wanderlog Pro offers offline maps, dark mode, AI suggestions).
**Vallecito Relevance:** Their itinerary UI (map + timeline) is the gold standard for travel planning. Vallecito can learn from their drag-and-drop reordering and collaborative aspects.

## Airbnb Experiences
**Focus:** High-quality, unique, host-led local experiences.
**Target Audience:** Travelers seeking authentic, non-touristy activities.
**Key Features:** Visually stunning listings, verified host profiles, seamless booking and messaging flow, integrated reviews.
**Monetization:** Commission-based (Airbnb takes a ~20% cut from the host).
**Vallecito Relevance:** Their UI/UX for displaying experience cards, host profiles, and reviews is top-tier. Vallecito aims to replicate this level of trust and visual polish for local Sacred Valley agencies.

## GetYourGuide & Viator
**Focus:** Aggregating standard tours and tickets globally.
**Target Audience:** Mass-market tourists looking to quickly book popular attractions.
**Key Features:** High volume of reviews, instant confirmation, mobile ticketing, flexible cancellation.
**Monetization:** Commission-based (usually 20-30% from the operator).
**Vallecito Relevance:** They represent the "status quo" OTA (Online Travel Agency) that Vallecito aims to disrupt by offering a more curated, local-first, low-commission alternative (S/5 claim fee + subscription).

## TripAdvisor
**Focus:** Reviews, discovery, and meta-search for travel.
**Target Audience:** Travelers in the discovery and validation phase of planning.
**Key Features:** Massive user-generated content (reviews, photos), forum discussions, price comparisons.
**Monetization:** Advertising, CPC (cost-per-click) leads to OTAs, and direct bookings (via Viator).
**Vallecito Relevance:** Demonstrates the power of trust and social proof. Vallecito's shadow lead generation and business claiming flow must leverage similar trust mechanics.
